How it works

Three steps, and the crowd never leaves their seat.

STEP ONE

Open your link — no app, no login

Display your crowd page QR code from the stage, or share the link straight up. It opens straight to your curated song list. Fans choose a song, add a tip and an optional note.

A tip-only option is available, too — for the fan that just wants to say thanks without requesting anything.

STEP TWO

Tips rank the queue, live, for everyone to see

Every tip lands straight in your own account and moves that song up. Anyone can add another tip to boost a song that's already in queue adding a social element.

If you've set a nightly cap, an already-full song just says so, warmly — no error, no dead end.

STEP THREE

You run the whole show from one tablet

Before you play: pick tonight's setlist and go live. During the set: the same tablet swipes through your own PDF charts, page by page — the request drawer waits quietly until you tap it. Play with a band? Every bandmate's tablet stays synced to the same spot.

Accept a request and its key, capo notes, and tip land right where you're already reading — the chart drops in as your very next swipe. Tap "Got it — play the song" and keep going.
