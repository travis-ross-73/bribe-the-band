How it works

Five steps, and no one's ever stuck without a chart.

STEP ONE

Open your link — no app, no login

Display your crowd page QR code from the stage, or share the link straight up. It opens straight to tonight's curated song list — only the songs you're actually ready to play, so nobody requests something you don't know. Fans choose a song, add a tip and an optional note.

A tip-only option is available, too — for the fan that just wants to say thanks without requesting anything.

STEP TWO

Tips rank the queue, live, for everyone to see

Every tip lands straight in your own account and moves that song up. Anyone can add another tip to boost a song that's already in queue — the crowd isn't just watching the set get built, they're building it.

If you've set a nightly cap, an already-full song just says so, warmly — no error, no dead end.

STEP THREE

The chart's already there when you accept

Before you play: pick tonight's setlist and go live. During the set: the same tablet swipes through your own PDF charts, page by page — the request drawer waits quietly until you tap it. No stopping to dig through a binder or a different app.

Accept a request and its key, capo notes, and tip land right where you're already reading — the chart drops in as your very next swipe. Tap "Got it — play the song" and keep going.

STEP FOUR

Playing with a band? Everyone stays in sync

Every bandmate follows tonight's live set from their own account, on their own device — no shared login, no passing one tablet around. The moment you accept a request, it splices into everyone's set at the same instant.

That sync is the actual point — nobody's guessing what's next, nobody's a beat behind. No more shouting a title across the stage and waiting for everyone to find their part.

STEP FIVE

Last Call, so the night ends clean

Tell it when tonight's set actually wraps, and requests taper off and close on their own as the clock runs out — no more accepting a request you'll never get to play, and no more standing on stage explaining why.

Completely optional — leave it blank and nothing about tonight's gig changes.
