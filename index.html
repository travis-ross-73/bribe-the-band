Song requests, tips, and charts all at your fingertips in real-time as you perform.

Bribe The Band turns your crowd's phones into a request line and a tip jar — with a live, tip-weighted queue and swipe-through PDF charts that run right on your tablet and sync with your band all while you play.

Three steps. No app for the crowd.

STEP ONE

Share your link

Display your crowd page QR code from the stage or share the link straight up. No app to download, no login for the crowd. Only show them the songs you're ready to perform.

STEP TWO

They request, and tip

The crowd picks a song, leaves a note, tips what it's worth. Other fans can boost it to move it up in the queue. Money lands straight in your own account — nothing pools, nothing waits.

STEP THREE

You run the queue

Requests stack up tip-weighted on your tablet with the chart already attached. Swipe through your own PDF charts the same way you already do — the requests wait until you tap it. It then drops the requested song's chart as your next swipe.

Built on an actual stage, not a whiteboard.

Bribe The Band started as one performer's fix for his own gigs — replacing a paper songbook and a literal tip jar with something his crowd could use from their phones and he could run from his tablet without having to say no to songs he didn't know or manually search for songs he did. He's been using it for both his solo gigs as well as his 5-piece band. It's been bringing in real requests and real tips at real shows since August 2026.

Free to sign up. We take 10% of tips — no monthly fee, nothing else.
