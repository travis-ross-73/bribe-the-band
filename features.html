Performer console

Step 1 — Go Live: pick a saved setlist, optionally override tonight's display name, then Start New Gig — one tap makes the crowd page live.

Step 2 — Perform: opens the chart viewer, the screen you actually swipe through and perform from.

Set a max requests per song and a cooldown after playing — both carry over from your last gig automatically.

Followers — a bandmate joins your gig with their own account and a join code; their tablet mirrors the same charts and set order without ever touching the queue.

Request Activity keeps a running tally of everything requested tonight — refresh any time to catch up.

Swipeable chart viewer

Your own PDF chords load right in — swipe, tap either side of the screen, or use the arrow keys to turn the page.

A quiet notification bell shows how many requests are waiting — the drawer only opens when you tap it, never mid-song.

Each request shows its key, tip, and a link to open the chart before you decide — Accept or Decline, right there.

Accepting drops that song's chart in as your very next swipe — then "Got it — play the song" and keep going.

A bandmate's Follower tablet stays synced to the same spot the moment you accept — everyone's charts move together.
